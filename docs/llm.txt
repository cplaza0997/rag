Large Language Models (LLMs) are advanced artificial intelligence systems trained on vast amounts of text data. They are capable of understanding, generating, and manipulating human language in a coherent and context-aware manner.

Popular LLMs include OpenAI's GPT series, Meta's LLaMA models, and Google's PaLM. These models are used in applications such as chatbots, translation, summarization, code generation, and much more.

LLMs rely on architectures like Transformers, which allow them to process input data in parallel and capture long-range dependencies within text.
